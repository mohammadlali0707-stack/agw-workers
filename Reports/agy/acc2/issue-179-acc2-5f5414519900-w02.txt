### Key Findings

#### 1. Root Cause in Run 45158355500 (Exit Code 3)
- **Hard Abort in Loop**: At `10:49:00Z` (~6.5 minutes into polling), Google displayed a sign-in challenge. In the previous implementation of [tools/gemini_mint.py](file:///home/runner/work/_temp/agent-repo-45166599659-1-run-agy/tools/gemini_mint.py), detecting a challenge triggered `fail("CAPTCHA_DETECTED: Google showed a challenge; stopping without touching the secret", code=3)`.
- **Architectural Mismatch**: While automated scraping routines halt on CAPTCHA, the mint workflow specifically provisions a virtual desktop (`Xvfb`), `x11vnc`, and `noVNC` over Cloudflare tunnels so the human operator can manually sign in and solve challenges. The immediate abort defeated this remote interactive model.
- **Automation Markers**: Chrome was launched without `--disable-blink-features=AutomationControlled`, leaving `navigator.webdriver = true` and exposing CDP automation hooks directly to Google bot heuristics over proxy egress `159.54.180.96`.
